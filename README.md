# Restaurant Franchise Food-Cost Rebate Recovery

Capture food and packaging rebates earned across franchise purchasing volume and approved supplier programs.

**Primary buyer:** Restaurant franchisors and multi-unit operators. **Evidence:** supplier agreements, approved products, distributor purchases, store sales, volume tiers, marketing allowances, quality credits, rebate statements, and cash.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Supplier program library
- Approved product registry
- Franchise location mapping
- Distributor purchase ingestion
- Net eligible volume
- Volume tier calculation
- Growth incentive
- Marketing allowance
- Quality shortfall credit
- Return spoilage exclusion
- Rebate accrual ledger
- Supplier statement audit
- Dispute workflow
- Cash credit reconciliation
- Brand supplier analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4736>. API: `5736`.

Administrator: `runtime-admin@example.com` / `LocalDemo!2026`. Operator and reviewer credential buttons are available on the login page.
